      WELCOME TO TRIVIA TOWER

      Six things. One correct order.

      Use your knowledge and a little instinct to build the daily tower.

      1. Read the question. Put the smallest, earliest, or lowest item at the bottom (1), and the largest, latest, or highest at the top (6).
      2. Build your tower. Drag the rows, tap their arrow buttons, or focus a row and use your keyboard arrows.
      3. Lock it in. You get one submission per difficulty each day. Your tower moves left and the answers appear on the right, one row at a time.
      4. Score every correct spot. Each exact placement counts, even if another row is wrong. Time never affects your score.
      Easy100 points / spot
      Medium150 points / spot
      Hard200 points / spot
