      WELCOME TO TRIVIA TOWER

      Six things. One correct order.

      Finish Easy, then Medium, then Hard. Your points from all three levels add up to one daily score.

      1. Read the question. Put the smallest, earliest, or lowest item at the bottom (1), and the largest, latest, or highest at the top (6).
      2. Build your tower. Drag the rows, tap their arrow buttons, or focus a row and use your keyboard arrows.
      3. Lock it in. You get one submission per level each day. Your tower moves left and the answers appear on the right. Finish the reveal to continue to the next level; you do not need a perfect score.
      4. Score every correct spot. Each exact placement earns one sixth of the total, even if another row is wrong. Scores round to whole points. Time never affects your score.
      Easy600 points max
      Medium800 points max
      Hard1,200 points max

      Up to 2,600 points each day. The leaderboard combines all three levels.
