HOW TO PLAY

Find the links.

Each neighboring pair makes a familiar phrase or compound word. For example:

CHAIN↕REACTION↕TIME

The first and last words are given. Fill the five missing rows from either end. Use ↑ or ↓ to switch ends. Every submitted guess counts; a wrong answer reveals one more letter. Fewer guesses make a better score.
